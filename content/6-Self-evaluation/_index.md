### **Areas for Improvement (Needs Improvement)**

* **Applied Production Knowledge & Architectural Depth:** While I demonstrated strong learning agility and problem-solving capability during development, transitioning from academic concepts to enterprise-grade cloud engineering requires deeper hands-on mastery of production trade-offs. Moving forward, I need to strengthen my practical proficiency in advanced cloud operations—specifically evaluating DynamoDB schema immutability, IAM least-privilege boundaries, token/quota throttling strategies, and cost optimization models (such as on-demand vs. provisioned capacity) upfront rather than refining them iteratively during implementation.
* **Engineering & Operational Discipline:** Although general workplace policies and attendance were respected, I need stricter self-discipline regarding standardized software engineering workflows. In particular, I must maintain consistent Git branching and commit conventions, enforce routine AWS budget and credential hygiene audits, and follow documented sprint schedules rigorously so that end-to-end integration testing and Terraform state verification are completed well ahead of final deployment deadlines.
* **Structured Technical Escalation:** While daily peer communication and problem-solving were effective, I can further improve how I package complex infrastructure bottlenecks (such as third-party API limits or service quota constraints) into concise, decision-ready proposals with clear cost and timeline impacts for mentors and stakeholders.

---

### **Action Plan & Remediation Strategies**

1. **Checklist-Driven Engineering Discipline:** Adopt a mandatory pre-PR verification checklist covering IAM least-privilege policies, branch naming compliance, Terraform plan validation, and cost impact checks before submitting code for review.
2. **Production Architecture & Service Deep-Dives:** Dedicate structured weekly study blocks to reviewing official AWS Well-Architected Framework pillars (Security, Reliability, and Cost Optimization) alongside hands-on labs to bridge the gap between functional prototypes and production-hardened systems.
3. **Milestone-Anchored Integration Testing:** Enforce internal buffer deadlines 48 hours prior to sprint reviews to complete cross-service integration testing and CloudWatch log verification, preventing last-minute deployment bottlenecks.
