During my 12-week internship in the **Workforce Bootcamp – First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program at **Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited** (14/09/2026 – 04/12/2026), I gained valuable hands-on experience in cloud computing, serverless architecture, and real-world team collaboration. Below are my honest reflections and constructive feedback for the program.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment at the AWS Vietnam office (Bitexco Financial Tower) and within the FCAJ community is open, welcoming, and energizing. Fellow interns, mentors, and community builders are approachable and willing to discuss technical challenges—whether during lab sessions, weekly meetups, or project reviews. Having access to a focused, collaborative workspace helped me stay disciplined and engaged throughout the 12 weeks.

**2. Support from Mentors & Program Organizers**  
Mentors and organizers provided clear learning roadmaps, structured milestones, and timely technical feedback without simply handing out ready-made answers. Whenever our team ran into blockers—such as IAM permissions boundaries, API throttling, or architecture trade-offs in ReLoop—mentors encouraged us to inspect AWS documentation, verify logs, and reason through the root cause ourselves. That guidance built real engineering independence.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
As an Information Assurance (Cyber-Security) student at FPT University, the internship aligned closely with my academic background while expanding it into production cloud engineering. Concepts like the AWS Shared Responsibility Model, least-privilege IAM roles, private S3 buckets with CloudFront OAC, Cognito JWT verification, AWS WAF rules, and Bedrock Guardrails against prompt injection let me apply security principles directly to a live serverless architecture.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
The program offered a well-rounded mix of self-paced AWS labs, technical writing, community events, and capstone project work. Publishing three technical articles for the AWS Study Group (on S3 Lifecycle, AWS Savings Plans, and Aurora Serverless v2) sharpened my ability to study official AWS documentation and explain cloud topics clearly. Participating in events such as the Buildrathon Kickoff, AWS Cloud & AI Day Watch Party, Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner Vogels, Weekly Meet Up, and Cloud Mastery broadened my industry perspective far beyond classroom coursework.

**5. Community Culture & Team Spirit**  
What stood out most was the AWS Study Group's *"Pay It Forward"* culture. Everyone—from senior engineers and Community Builders to fellow interns—shared practical lessons openly and respectfully. Within our 5-person team (**AWSS3nt1n4L**), collaborating across architecture review, infrastructure inventory, security testing, and documentation taught me how much clear communication and mutual accountability matter when shipping a complete product.

**6. Program Structure & Benefits**  
The flexible hybrid schedule, access to AWS learning resources, Free Tier / credit guidance, and frequent exposure to AWS engineers and partner companies (such as CMC Global, Revve AI, Styl Solution, and ACB) made the internship genuinely rewarding. The structured weekly worklog, blog milestones, and workshop documentation deliverables kept our progress measurable from start to finish.

---

### Additional Questions

* **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
  Seeing how individual AWS services connect into one cohesive, real-world serverless system—from CloudFront + WAF at the edge down to Cognito, API Gateway, Lambda, DynamoDB, S3, Rekognition, and Bedrock—and being able to review, verify, and document that entire architecture for ReLoop. Attending the Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner Vogels and connecting with builders at the weekly meetups were also unforgettable highlights.

* **What do you think the program should improve for future interns?**  
  During Week 1, AWS account registration and payment card verification can occasionally take longer than expected, delaying hands-on console practice. Providing a short pre-onboarding checklist (covering supported payment cards, common verification pitfalls, and fallback sandbox exercises while waiting for activation) would help new interns hit the ground running faster.

* **If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**  
  **Yes, definitely.** FCAJ is one of the best programs in Vietnam for students who want real exposure to AWS Cloud, modern AI engineering, and cloud security rather than purely theoretical exercises. Anyone who is proactive and willing to learn independently will gain both strong technical depth and a supportive community network.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

* **Suggestions to improve the internship experience:**  
  1. Share a brief **pre-Week 1 AWS account setup guide** before the official start date so interns can resolve card verification delays early.  
  2. Include a short **team-formation & Git/Terraform workflow kickoff** early in the internship so new teams align on branching conventions, IAM user hygiene, and cost monitoring from day one.  
  3. Continue hosting hands-on **Weekly Meet Ups** and **Cloud Mastery** sessions—they bridge the gap between university studies and enterprise engineering very effectively.

* **Would you like to continue with this community in the future?**  
  Yes. Even after completing the 12-week internship, I would love to stay active in the **AWS Study Group**, continue sharing technical notes and security-focused cloud articles, and support future FCAJ cohorts in the spirit of *"Pay It Forward"*.

* **Closing Note:**  
  I am sincerely grateful to the AWS Vietnam team, FCAJ mentors, program organizers, and my teammates in **AWSS3nt1n4L** for their guidance, patience, and encouragement throughout this journey. The skills, discipline, and mindset I built during these 12 weeks will stay with me as I pursue a career in Cloud Security and Cloud Engineering.
